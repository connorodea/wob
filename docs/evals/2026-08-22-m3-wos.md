# M3 WOS vs Lowest-Price Ranking Evaluation

- **Date**: 2026-08-22
- **Model**: `wob/scoring.py` WOS v1 (transparent weighted sum, no learned weights)
- **Dataset**: `tests/fixtures/deal_quality/2026-08-22.jsonl` — 15 hand-labeled
  candidates (9 good / 6 not-good), including traps: cheap-but-wrecked
  condition, cheap-but-irrelevant, relevant-but-overpriced
- **Method**: `wob/woseval.py` — NDCG@8 + top-8 precision, binary gains

| Ranker | NDCG@8 | Precision@8 |
|---|---|---|
| WOS v1 | **0.995** | **0.875** |
| lowest landed price | 0.486 | 0.375 |

WOS beats the naive price ranking on both metrics: price alone puts the
$1.50 junk novel and the wrecked textbook on top.
